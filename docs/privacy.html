Privacy Policy for Ballgame

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Sialm Studio ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Ballgame mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policy regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

No Data Collection

Ballgame does not transmit, upload, store on our servers, or share any personal data with us or any third party. The App does not require account creation or login, and does not collect analytics, usage data, device identifiers, location data, or any other personal information from its users.

Photo Library Access

Ballgame may request access to your photo library so that you can attach photos to the games you've logged. Any photos you choose to add are stored locally on your device as part of the App's data and are used only for this purpose. Photos are never uploaded, transmitted off your device, stored on our servers, or shared with any third party.

Third-Party Services

Ballgame does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Because Ballgame does not collect any personal data, it does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13, or any other age, consistent with applicable law.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
